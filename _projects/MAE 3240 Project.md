**Summary of Project**

I chose this problem since it represents a wide breath of concepts learned in this course. The beginning parts focus on more straightfoward heat transfer problems, such as finding the temperature at a spot on the singular fin using the solved fin equation for an insulated tip. Then, the problem progresses to solve for the thermal ressitance network, fin effectivelness and fin efficiency. Now, the concept of a fin array comes into play, with the addition of a 5 x 5 array. From there, the importance of a heat sink is seen by calculating the temperature difference between the addition of the fin array versus the heater exposed to convection.\

This problem clearly showed me the progression of solving complex heat sink problems, starting with the fin and progressing to an array on a heater. This helped me understand how to solve a more complex problem, such as part (f) on its own. Also, dividing the problem into multiple parts helps solidify the changes adding fins and an array of such to a heater. For example, in the end, calculating the temperature difference of adding the fin array as a heat sink to the heater verus the heater exposed to convection only shows that there is significant differences in operation just by using an extended surface.
